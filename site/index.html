    How to play

    • Roll six dice. Tap the dice that score, then roll the rest or bank.
    • 1 is 100, 5 is 50. Three of a kind is face x 100 (three 1s is 1000). Four, five or six of a kind: 1000, 2000, 3000.
    • 1-2-3-4-5-6, three pairs, or four of a kind plus a pair: 1500. Two triples: 2500.
    • If a roll has nothing that scores, that is a Farkel and the turn's points are lost.
    • Score with all six dice for hot dice: roll all six again.
    • You need 500 in one turn to get on the board. First to 10,000 triggers one last turn for everyone else.
